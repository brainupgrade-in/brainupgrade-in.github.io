What is Cloud Labs?

Cloud Labs is a hands-on lab service for corporate training. Every participant gets their own ready-to-use sandbox in the browser: Kubernetes, Linux, Docker, Python on GPU, Agentic AI or Claude Code. Nothing is installed and no VPN is needed. Labs are built for each cohort, monitored throughout the session and deleted afterwards.

Run by Gheware Technologies, Bengaluru, India · Founder and lead trainer: Rajesh Gheware · Updated 12 November 2026

Why teams come to us

Hands-on training fails in the first hour, not the last

The course content is rarely the problem. The lab is. These are the three things we hear most from training managers and trainers.

“Half the room spent the morning installing tools.”

Corporate laptops block installs, Docker and admin rights. Our labs need only a browser: if the laptop can open a website, it can open the lab.

“One broken environment stalled the whole class.”

Each participant has their own sandbox. One person breaking their cluster affects nobody else, and a restart brings it back with their work intact.

“We can't give 40 people a cloud account or an AI key.”

You don't have to. We run the cloud, the GPUs and the AI models. Every AI lab comes with its own key and a daily spending cap, already set up.

For your IT and security team

The questions your IT team will ask, answered up front

🌐

Browser only

HTTPS in a normal browser. Nothing is installed on participant laptops and nothing connects to your network.

🧱

Isolated per person

Each participant has their own sandbox, namespace, permissions and resource limits. One person cannot see or break another's work.

🔒

Kept off private networks

AI lab sandboxes can reach the internet for packages, but network policy blocks private address ranges, including other sandboxes.

🗑️

Nothing left behind

Each cohort gets a fresh environment and fresh passwords. It is deleted after the session.

💳

Capped AI spend

Every AI key has a daily cap per participant. A mistake in a loop stops at the cap instead of turning into a bill.

🧾

No accounts for your people

Participants don't need a cloud account, a GitHub login or an AI subscription. We provide the infrastructure.

FAQ

Common questions

How much notice do you need?

Tell us the date as early as you can. For a standard lab and a confirmed head-count, labs can be ready within 24 hours. A custom lab image or a new course needs more time. We confirm what's possible within one business day.

How many participants can you handle?

Anything from a single class to 100+ at once. Our largest single cohort so far was 135 sandboxes running at the same time.

Will it work on our corporate laptops?

If the laptop can open a normal HTTPS website, it can open the lab. There is nothing to install and no VPN. If your proxy restricts new domains, we can send your IT team the addresses to allow in advance.

What happens if a participant breaks their sandbox?

It only affects them. The trainer can see their namespace to help, and a restart brings the sandbox back with their work intact. Our monitoring usually spots and fixes failures before anyone raises a hand.

Is the work kept between days of a multi-day course?

Yes. Each participant has persistent storage for the length of the course. Everything is deleted when the cohort ends.

Do participants need their own AI or cloud accounts?

No. The AI labs come with a per-participant key that already works, with a daily spending cap. The cloud and GPU infrastructure is ours.

Can you use our own course material or exercises?

Yes. We can pre-install your tools and clone your repository into every sandbox, so participants start on your first exercise. Tell us what you need in the form below.

How is it priced?

Per cohort, based on the lab type, number of participants and number of days. Send us those three and we'll come back with a quote.
